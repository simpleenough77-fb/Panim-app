# Contributing to Panim

Panim is free, open source, noncommercial and ad-free, built for people who want simpler technology. Contributions are welcome, but the product is intentionally small.

- Read `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` first: they list the product rules (no accounts, backend, analytics, ads, tracking or third-party dependencies unless a recorded decision says so; minimal permissions; accessibility as an acceptance criterion; never log contact data).
- Open an issue to discuss before large changes. Keep pull requests small, with tests and any needed documentation.
- Never commit secrets or real personal data; use synthetic fixtures.
- Accessibility changes must say how they were checked (device, OS, assistive technology) and what remains unverified.
- Contributions are accepted under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`, section 5). The Panim name, logo and brand assets are not covered by that license; see `NOTICE`.
- Proposed (not yet adopted): a Panim-specific commit/PR convention and a Code of Conduct.
